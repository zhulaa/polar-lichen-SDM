# Trait-mediated climatic filtering shapes the spatial futures of polar lichens


This repository contains R scripts for modelling polar lichen climatic suitability under current and future climate scenarios.



## Workflow

1. Data cleaning and spatial thinning

2. Environmental variable extraction

3. Heatmap of the correlations among the retained environmental factors

4. Ensemble SDM modelling and future projuection

5. The contribution rate of environmental factors to the ensemble model

6. Thermal response curves_Antarctica

7. Thermal response curves_Arctic

8. Projected changes and spatial patterns of climatically suitable habitat for polar lichens under future climate scenarios.
9.  Redistribution of climatically suitable habitats along latitudinal and elevational gradients



## R control

R 4.4.2 



## Data resource

The trait-attributed lichen datasets were obtained from Alatan et al. and is available from the corresponding repository via Zenodo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14236370. 

The NSIDC data are available at https://doi.org/10.5067/B8X58MQBFUPA. 

The current and Future climate predictor layers were obtained from WorldClim version 2.1 and are available at https://www.worldclim.org/data/worldclim21.html. 

The elevation data are available at https://www.worldclim.org/data/worldclim21.html. 

Processed climatic suitability projections and raster outputs generated in this analysis are available through Science DB at https://doi.org/10.57760/sciencedb.013cl.
